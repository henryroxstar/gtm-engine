usage: python -m gtm_core.video_finish [-h]
                                       {run,predictor-trim,grade,frames-to-video,mux,split,stitch,burn-captions,overlays,overlay-scene,find-transient,mix-sfx,room-tone,voice-polish,narration-track,contact-sheet,reframe} ...

positional arguments:
  {run,predictor-trim,grade,frames-to-video,mux,split,stitch,burn-captions,overlays,overlay-scene,find-transient,mix-sfx,room-tone,voice-polish,narration-track,contact-sheet,reframe}
    grade               apply ONE eq grade to one shot, audio stream-copied
                        (per-shot assembly path)
    frames-to-video     encode a numbered PNG frame sequence
                        (gtm_core.screen_ui output) into a silent mp4
    mux                 attach one VO track to one silent rendered shot
                        (multi-shot render, per shot)
    split               cut one continuous take into its per-beat files (the
                        inverse of stitch)
    stitch              concatenate finished per-shot files into one asset
                        (hard cut by default)
    burn-captions       burn each shot's caption into that shot's own file,
                        BEFORE the stitch
    overlays            draw each shot's production.overlay chat bubble beside
                        the phone and composite it into that shot's own file,
                        BEFORE the stitch
    overlay-scene       composite an RGBA PNG sequence (a gtm_core.screen_ui
                        overlay scene) over one shot at 1:1, keeping the
                        shot's own audio
    find-transient      where a cue file's sound actually starts (relative
                        level sweep, not silencedetect)
    mix-sfx             mix N timed SFX cues onto a shot's existing audio
                        (normalize=0, verified by measurement)
    room-tone           synthesize a low room-tone bed (.m4a) — the noise
                        floor, never a score
    voice-polish        clean up one narration take: highpass, de-mud,
                        presence lift, de-ess, gentle compression — lossless
                        WAV, duration-preserving
    narration-track     place a shot list's `narration` read onto one voice-
                        only master (.m4a). Refuses if the lane fails its
                        lint, or if a line's `len_s` disagrees with its audio.
    contact-sheet       evenly spaced frames as one image, for looking at a
                        finished asset
    reframe             crop OS chrome off a raw screen recording, then pad it
                        to an exact platform ratio

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
