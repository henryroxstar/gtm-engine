  Errors block delivery. Warnings do not, but they are graded: read them,
  then either fix or say why not, and where one is genuinely wrong about a
  document suppress it with <!-- lint-ok SD4: reason --> in that section.
  Judgement calls (~) are neither — they block nothing, --strict does not
  promote them, and they take no lint-ok. Three tiers, three answers: two
  that contradict each other train a reader to ignore both.
